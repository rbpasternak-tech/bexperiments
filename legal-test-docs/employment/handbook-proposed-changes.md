# PROPOSED HANDBOOK CHANGES — Q3 2026

**Prepared by:** HR Department
**Date:** May 10, 2026
**For Review By:** Employment Counsel

---

## Change 1: PTO Policy Update

**Current (Section 7.1):** PTO accrues per pay period. Carry over up to 5 days; excess forfeited on January 1.

**Proposed:** Replace the carry-over/forfeiture model with a "use-it-or-lose-it" policy. Employees must use all PTO by December 31 of each year. No carry-over. Unused PTO is forfeited.

**Rationale:** Reduce PTO liability on the balance sheet. Multiple employees are banking PTO and taking extended absences in Q4.

---

## Change 2: Remote Work Restrictions

**Current (Section 5.2):** Remote work approved case-by-case by direct manager.

**Proposed:** Effective August 1, 2026, all employees must work from a Company office at least 4 days per week. Remote work is limited to 1 day per week and requires VP-level approval. Employees who were hired as fully remote must relocate to within commuting distance of a Company office within 90 days or their position will be eliminated.

**Rationale:** CEO believes in-person collaboration drives innovation. Several remote employees have been less responsive.

---

## Change 3: Arbitration Clause Expansion

**Current (Section 14):** Mandatory binding arbitration for all employment disputes. Class/collective action waiver included.

**Proposed:** Add the following: "This arbitration agreement also covers claims arising under the National Labor Relations Act, claims related to workplace safety, and claims for unpaid wages. Employees waive any right to file a charge with or participate in any investigation by any federal, state, or local administrative agency, including but not limited to the EEOC, NLRB, OSHA, and state equivalents."

**Rationale:** Recent employee filed EEOC charge; legal wants to prevent future filings.

---

## Change 4: Social Media Policy Tightening

**Current (Section 13):** Employees should exercise good judgment. No disclosure of confidential info. No speaking on behalf of Company without authorization.

**Proposed:** Add: "Employees may not post negative or disparaging comments about the Company, its management, products, or working conditions on any social media platform or online forum, whether public or private. This includes but is not limited to Glassdoor, Reddit, Twitter/X, LinkedIn, and private Slack communities. Violation will result in immediate termination."

**Rationale:** Negative Glassdoor reviews have affected recruiting. HR wants to deter.

---

## Change 5: Expense Reimbursement Timeline

**Current:** Not explicitly addressed in handbook.

**Proposed:** "Employees must submit expense reports within 14 calendar days of incurring the expense. Expenses submitted after 14 days will not be reimbursed, regardless of amount or prior approval."

**Rationale:** Finance team is dealing with stale expense reports from months ago.

---

## Change 6: Lactation Accommodation

**Current:** Not addressed in handbook.

**Proposed:** "The Company will provide a reasonable break time and a private space (other than a bathroom) for employees who need to express breast milk for a nursing child for up to one year after the child's birth. Employees should coordinate break times with their manager to minimize disruption to workflow."

**Rationale:** New employee asked about this; realized we have no written policy. Drafting to comply with PUMP Act requirements.
