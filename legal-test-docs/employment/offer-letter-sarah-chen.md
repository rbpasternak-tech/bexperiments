## Benefits

You will be eligible for the Company's standard benefits package, including medical, dental, and vision insurance, effective on your start date. Details will be provided during onboarding.

## Employment Classification

This position is classified as **exempt** under the Fair Labor Standards Act. Your work schedule is Monday through Friday, with an expectation of approximately 50-55 hours per week to meet the demands of the role.

## At-Will Employment

Your employment with the Company is at-will, meaning either you or the Company may terminate the employment relationship at any time, for any reason, with or without cause or notice.

## Non-Competition Agreement

As a condition of your employment, you agree that during your employment and for a period of **24 months** following the termination of your employment for any reason, you will not, directly or indirectly:

1. Engage in, own, manage, operate, or provide services to any business that competes with the Company's products or services within the **United States**;
2. Solicit, recruit, or hire any employee or contractor of the Company;
3. Solicit or divert any customer, client, or business partner of the Company.

This non-competition obligation applies regardless of the reason for termination, including termination without cause or constructive dismissal. You acknowledge that this restriction is reasonable and necessary to protect the Company's legitimate business interests.

## Confidentiality and Invention Assignment

You agree to sign the Company's standard Confidentiality, Invention Assignment, and Non-Disclosure Agreement (CIANDA) on or before your start date. All inventions, discoveries, and work product created during your employment, whether or not created during business hours or using Company resources, shall be the sole property of the Company.

This offer is contingent upon successful completion of a background check, including criminal history, education verification, and credit check.
