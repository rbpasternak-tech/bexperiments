## Nature of Complaint

Jennifer alleges the following:

### Incident 1 — March 15, 2026 (Team Happy Hour)

At a company-sponsored happy hour at a bar near the office, Ryan made repeated comments about Jennifer's appearance, telling her she "looked amazing" and "should dress like that more often at work." He put his arm around her shoulder multiple times while taking group photos and insisted on sitting next to her. Jennifer states she felt uncomfortable but laughed it off because he is her direct supervisor.

### Incident 2 — April 3, 2026 (One-on-One Meeting)

During a scheduled one-on-one in Ryan's office (door closed), Ryan allegedly told Jennifer that she had "real potential" and that he could "fast-track her promotion" if she was "willing to put in the extra time with him." When Jennifer asked what he meant, he said "I think you know" and smiled. Jennifer states she felt this was a quid pro quo proposition but was not sure. She did not respond and the conversation moved to other topics.

### Incident 3 — April 15, 2026 (Slack DM)

Ryan sent Jennifer a Slack DM at 11:47 PM on a Saturday: "Hey, thinking about you. That presentation you gave was really something. We should celebrate — dinner sometime?" Jennifer did not respond. On Monday, Ryan asked her in the hallway if she got his message and seemed irritated that she hadn't replied.

### Incident 4 — April 22, 2026 (Performance Context)

Jennifer was notified that she was being removed from the high-profile Q3 product launch campaign and reassigned to routine social media scheduling. She was told by Ryan that the decision was based on "bandwidth" concerns, but no other team members were reassigned. Jennifer believes this is retaliation for not responding to Ryan's Slack message and for being visibly distant from him in the office.
