## 1. SCOPE OF WORK

Contractor will provide UX design services for the Company's product team, including:

- Creating wireframes, mockups, and prototypes for new product features
- Conducting user research and usability testing
- Participating in daily standup meetings and weekly design reviews
- Collaborating with product managers and engineers on feature specifications
- Maintaining and updating the Company's design system

## 2. WORK SCHEDULE AND LOCATION

Contractor will be available during core business hours (9:00 AM – 5:00 PM CT, Monday through Friday) and will work from the Company's Austin office at least 3 days per week. Contractor will use a Company-provided laptop, Company Figma account, and Company Slack workspace.

## 3. COMPENSATION

- **Rate:** $75/hour
- **Expected Hours:** 40 hours per week
- **Invoicing:** Contractor will submit weekly timesheets through the Company's time-tracking system (Harvest). Payment will be made within 30 days of approved timesheet submission.
- **Expenses:** The Company will reimburse pre-approved business expenses, including travel, software subscriptions, and conference attendance.

## 4. REPORTING

Contractor reports to **Alex Kim, Head of Design**, and will receive regular feedback through the Company's quarterly performance review process. Contractor's work priorities will be set by the product team through the Company's sprint planning process.

## 5. EQUIPMENT AND TOOLS

The Company will provide:
- MacBook Pro laptop
- External monitor
- Figma Enterprise license
- Access to all internal tools (Slack, Jira, Confluence, Google Workspace)
- Company email address (maria.martinez@acmetech.com)
- Building access badge

## 6. EXCLUSIVITY

During the term of this agreement, Contractor agrees to work exclusively for the Company and will not provide design services to any other client without prior written approval.

## 7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

All work product created by Contractor under this agreement is work-for-hire and shall be the sole property of the Company. Contractor assigns all rights, title, and interest in any inventions, designs, or creative works produced during the engagement.

## 8. BENEFITS

Contractor is not eligible for Company benefits, including health insurance, PTO, retirement plans, or equity grants. Contractor is responsible for their own taxes, insurance, and self-employment obligations.

## 9. TERMINATION

Either party may terminate this agreement with 2 weeks' written notice. The Company may terminate immediately for cause, including breach of confidentiality, failure to perform, or misconduct.

## 10. NON-SOLICITATION

For 12 months following termination, Contractor agrees not to solicit any employees or clients of the Company.

## 11. GOVERNING LAW

This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas. Disputes shall be resolved through binding arbitration in Austin, TX.
