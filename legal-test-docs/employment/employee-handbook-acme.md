# ACME TECHNOLOGIES, INC. — EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025
**Last Revised:** January 1, 2025

---

## 1. WELCOME

Welcome to Acme Technologies! This handbook outlines our policies and expectations. It is not a contract of employment and does not create any contractual rights. The Company reserves the right to modify these policies at any time.

## 2. EMPLOYMENT AT WILL

All employment at Acme Technologies is at-will. Either the employee or the Company may end the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause or notice. No manager or representative of the Company has the authority to enter into any agreement for employment for any specified period of time.

## 3. EQUAL EMPLOYMENT OPPORTUNITY

Acme Technologies is an equal opportunity employer. We do not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, or genetic information in any aspect of employment.

## 4. ANTI-HARASSMENT POLICY

Acme Technologies is committed to providing a workplace free from harassment. Harassment based on any protected characteristic is prohibited. Employees who experience or witness harassment should report it to their manager or HR.

Reports will be investigated promptly. Retaliation against anyone who reports harassment in good faith is prohibited.

## 5. WORK HOURS AND ATTENDANCE

### 5.1 Standard Hours

The standard work week is Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM local time. Exempt employees are expected to work the hours necessary to fulfill their responsibilities, which may exceed 40 hours per week.

### 5.2 Remote Work

Remote work may be approved on a case-by-case basis by your direct manager. The Company reserves the right to require in-office attendance at any time with reasonable notice.

### 5.3 Attendance

Employees are expected to report to work as scheduled. Three or more unexcused absences in a 90-day period may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination.

## 6. COMPENSATION

### 6.1 Pay Periods

Employees are paid semi-monthly on the 15th and last day of each month. If a payday falls on a weekend or holiday, payment will be made on the preceding business day.

### 6.2 Overtime

Non-exempt employees are entitled to overtime pay at 1.5x their regular rate for hours worked over 40 in a workweek. Overtime must be pre-approved by your manager. Working unauthorized overtime may result in disciplinary action; however, all hours worked will be compensated.

### 6.3 Deductions

The Company may deduct from an exempt employee's salary for full-day absences for personal reasons other than sickness, and for absences due to sickness after the employee has exhausted paid sick leave.

## 7. PAID TIME OFF (PTO)

### 7.1 PTO Accrual

| Tenure | Annual PTO |
|---|---|
| 0-2 years | 15 days |
| 3-5 years | 20 days |
| 6+ years | 25 days |

PTO accrues on a per-pay-period basis. Employees may carry over up to 5 days of unused PTO into the following calendar year. Any PTO beyond 5 days will be forfeited on January 1.

### 7.2 PTO Requests

PTO requests should be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance for planned absences. PTO is subject to manager approval based on business needs.

### 7.3 PTO at Separation

Upon voluntary resignation with at least 2 weeks' notice, accrued but unused PTO will be paid out. PTO will not be paid out in cases of involuntary termination or resignation without adequate notice.

## 8. LEAVES OF ABSENCE

### 8.1 Family and Medical Leave (FMLA)

Eligible employees are entitled to up to 12 weeks of unpaid leave per year under the FMLA for qualifying reasons. Eligibility requires 12 months of employment and 1,250 hours worked in the preceding 12 months.

### 8.2 Other Leaves

Additional leaves (jury duty, bereavement, military) are provided in accordance with applicable law. Contact HR for details.

## 9. BENEFITS

### 9.1 Health Insurance

Medical, dental, and vision insurance is available to full-time employees and their dependents. Coverage begins on the first day of the month following 60 days of employment.

### 9.2 401(k)

The Company offers a 401(k) plan with a 3% employer match after 6 months of employment.

## 10. PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT

### 10.1 Performance Reviews

Performance reviews are conducted quarterly. Ratings are: Exceeds Expectations, Meets Expectations, Needs Improvement, and Does Not Meet Expectations.

### 10.2 Performance Improvement Plans

Employees who receive a "Does Not Meet Expectations" rating may be placed on a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP). PIPs are typically 60-90 days and include specific, measurable objectives.

## 11. DISCIPLINE AND TERMINATION

### 11.1 Progressive Discipline

The Company generally follows a progressive discipline process: verbal warning, written warning, final written warning, termination. However, the Company reserves the right to skip steps or proceed directly to termination depending on the severity of the conduct.

### 11.2 Termination

Upon termination, employees must return all Company property, including laptops, badges, and any confidential materials. Final paychecks will be issued in accordance with applicable state law.

## 12. CONFIDENTIALITY

Employees may have access to confidential information during their employment. This information must not be disclosed to anyone outside the Company without authorization. This obligation survives termination of employment.

## 13. SOCIAL MEDIA POLICY

Employees should exercise good judgment when using social media. Employees may not disclose confidential Company information, speak on behalf of the Company without authorization, or post content that is defamatory or harassing. Violation of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

## 14. ARBITRATION AGREEMENT

By continuing employment with the Company, all employees agree to resolve any and all disputes arising out of or related to their employment exclusively through binding arbitration. This includes claims under federal, state, or local law, including but not limited to claims of discrimination, harassment, retaliation, wrongful termination, and wage and hour violations. Employees waive their right to a jury trial and to participate in any class or collective action. The arbitration shall be conducted by a single arbitrator under the rules of the American Arbitration Association, and the Company shall pay all arbitration fees.
